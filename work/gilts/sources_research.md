# Gilts: external research (sources and exact quotes)

Accessed: 2026-10-29 (all rows unless stated). Case date: 9 May 2014. Prepared by sonnet-worker for the lead agent; nothing under materials/ or review/ was opened.

Conventions: dates are as printed at the source. DMO xls dates are Excel serial numbers in the file; I converted them to calendar dates (marked 'converted'). Years-to-maturity are my own arithmetic ((maturity - 9 May 2014)/365.25), marked 'calc'. 'Secondary' = not an official source; cross-check before it goes on a slide.

## Access notes

- WebFetch could not resolve dmo.gov.uk, but `curl` through the session proxy worked, so DMO PDFs/XLS were downloaded and parsed locally. DMO's live data reports (`/data/pdfdatareport?reportCode=D1A`, i.e. gilts in issue) are behind a bot-captcha and could NOT be read; the 2014 list was rebuilt from the DMO reference-price file (below), which is primary and complete for conventional gilts on 9 May 2014.
- archive.org / Wayback is not reachable. bloomberg.com, icaew.com, dividenddata.co.uk returned 403/404.

### A1. Verdict per case bond

Rule applied: CERTAIN = coupon matches AND maturity within about 1 year of the case tenor AND only one candidate. **None of the nine passes.** In May 2014 there was no gilt with the case's coupon at the case's tenor for 7 of the 9 bonds, so the case's coupons look stylised/hypothetical. Treat the case bonds as hypothetical bonds, and use real gilts only as market context.
